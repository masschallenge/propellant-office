# Google Slides

This guide covers presentations in Google Slides. Read the [foundations](foundations.md) first,
and the [PowerPoint guide](powerpoint.md) for layouts, titles, text and charts: the same rules
apply, and this page covers what is different in Google Slides.

## Start from the template

1. Download [`propellant-presentation.pptx`](../templates/powerpoint/propellant-presentation.pptx).
2. In Google Drive, choose **New**, then **File upload**, and pick it.
3. Open it and choose **File**, then **Save as Google Slides**. Work in the Google Slides copy.

If your organization has added Propellant to the Slides template gallery or made it the default
theme, use that instead. [Installing the templates](templates.md) explains both.

### Bringing an existing deck onto the brand

1. Open the deck and choose **Slide**, then **Change theme**.
2. Choose **Import theme** at the bottom of the panel, and pick the Propellant presentation from
   Drive or upload the `.pptx`.
3. Select the Propellant theme. Then select each slide and set its layout with **Slide**, then
   **Apply layout**.

Google does not publish a list of what changes when a PowerPoint file is converted. Check the
title slide, a section slide, a content slide and the closing slide after converting. The closing
slide carries the short About MassChallenge line and the contact and copyright lines, as the
[PowerPoint guide](powerpoint.md#the-closing-slide) explains.

## Slide size

Choose **File**, then **Page setup**, and keep **Widescreen 16:9**. A converted Propellant deck
keeps PowerPoint's 13.333 by 7.5 inch size, which is right; do not change it once you have
started, because the layouts will shift.

## Fonts and theme colors

- Lato and Oswald are available in Google Slides. If they are not in your font menu, choose
  **More fonts** and add them.
- Check the theme colors with **Slide**, then **Edit theme**, then **Colors**. The Propellant
  colors appear as Text and background, Accent 1 to 6 and Link. Pick colors from the theme
  section of any color menu, never a custom color.

## Editing the layouts

Google calls the master slides the **theme builder**: choose **Slide**, then **Edit theme**. The
first slide in the left panel is the master; the rest are the layouts. The converted template
already carries the Propellant master, with the Deep teal title and section layouts and the logo.
Change layouts here only to fix something for every slide, and keep the original Propellant theme
untouched in a separate copy.

## Photos and video

- Insert photos with **Insert**, then **Image**. To crop, double click the image and drag the
  black crop handles; to choose a shape, use the arrow beside the crop button.
- Add alt text: right click the image and choose **Alt text**.
- Insert video with **Insert**, then **Video**, from YouTube or Drive. Under **Format options**,
  set it to play on click and turn on captions if they exist.
- Follow the [photography guide](photography.md).

## Presenting and sharing

- Google Slides has no built-in accessibility checker. Check that every slide has a title (open
  **View**, then **Grid view**, to see them all), every image has alt text and the text is 18 pt
  or larger.
- Turn on captions while presenting with the **CC** button in the presenter toolbar.
- For people outside MassChallenge, share a view only link or download a PDF with **File**, then
  **Download**, then **PDF document**.
